Settings

Deliveries are kept only on this phone and are wiped automatically at the start of each new day. Photos and medication text are never saved. Exact house lookup sends the address text (not the name) to OpenStreetMap’s free Nominatim service; turn it off if that is not allowed, and MediRoute will use the postcode only. Address suggestions send the text you type to the free Photon service (Komoot) in the same way.
